# Weekly review

Draft only.
This skill reports and proposes, and it changes memory or creates reminders only after the person approves each item.

## Collect

1. Open loops from `MEMORY.md` and the past week's daily notes.
2. Commitments coming due, with their dates and whom they are owed to.
3. The week ahead on the calendar, when one is connected: deadlines, meetings that need prep, and clashes.
4. Follow-ups the person owes others, from notes, threads, and drafts.

## Report

- **Open loops:** oldest first, each with its next step.
- **Coming due:** commitments with dates.
- **Week ahead:** what needs preparation or attention.
- **Follow-ups owed:** each with a short draft where one would help.

Cite where each item came from.

## Propose

- **Memory updates:** entries to add, close, or correct in `MEMORY.md`, listed so the person can approve each one.
- **Reminders:** for each, what it covers and how often it runs.

Apply only what the person approves, and record each new reminder in the table in `TOOLS.md`.
