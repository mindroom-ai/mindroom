# Meeting prep

Draft only.
This skill reads sources and writes a brief, and it never sends, accepts, declines, or edits the invite.

## Gather

1. The invite: title, time, organizer, agenda, and anything attached, from the connected calendar or from what the person pastes.
2. Attendees: who each one is in this work context, from `USER.md`, `MEMORY.md`, and any configured directory.
3. Prior notes: earlier meetings in the series, related daily notes found with `search_memories`, and earlier briefs in the workspace.
4. Related open loops and commitments from `MEMORY.md`.
5. Linked documents: read what the invite and the prior notes point to.

When a source is unavailable, say so in the brief instead of filling the gap.

## Write the brief

Keep it to one screen, with these parts:

- **Goals:** what the meeting is for and what the person wants out of it.
- **Context:** the few facts that matter, each with its source and date.
- **Questions to raise:** open points worth asking about.
- **Decisions needed:** what has to be decided, and by whom.

End with a short list of the sources used.

## Afterwards

Offer to save the brief in the workspace and to add any follow-ups to today's daily note.
