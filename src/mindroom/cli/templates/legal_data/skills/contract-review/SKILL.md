# Contract review

Information, not advice.
This skill explains a document and points out what deserves attention; it never says a document is safe to sign.

## Read

1. Read the whole document, including schedules, annexes, and anything it incorporates by reference, and say which parts you could not see.
2. Note the document type, the parties, the date, the governing law, and where disputes go.
3. Check the `Jurisdiction:` line in `USER.md`, and say when the governing law points somewhere else.

## Explain

Summarize in plain language, quoting the clause number for each point:

- **What each side must do**, including money: amounts, when they are due, and what happens when they are late.
- **How long it lasts:** the term, renewal, and whether it renews automatically.
- **How it ends:** notice periods, termination rights for each side, and what survives the end.
- **Who carries the risk:** liability caps, indemnities, warranties, and insurance.
- **Limits on the person:** non-compete, non-solicitation, exclusivity, and confidentiality.
- **Rights they give up or grant:** intellectual property, use of their data, and changes the other side may make by notice.
- **Disputes:** arbitration, class-action waivers, and where and under which law a claim is heard.

## Flag

List clauses that are unusual, one-sided, or vague, each with its exact words and why it matters.
List every date and deadline the document sets, and count each one as `AGENTS.md` describes.

## Close

- **Questions to ask the other side**, one per flagged clause where one would help.
- **When to get a lawyer:** say so first if `AGENTS.md` calls for one.
- **Memory:** offer to save the summary and deadlines to the matter's file in `memory/matters/`, and save only what the person approves.
