# MEMORY.md

What is worth remembering about this person's money.
MindRoom loads this file on every turn, so keep it short, with raw notes in `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and monthly reviews in `memory/reviews/`.

- Start each entry with the date it was true, as `YYYY-MM-DD`, because balances and rates go stale.
- When two entries disagree, keep both, mark them `(conflict)`, and ask the person.
- Remove an entry once it is paid off, cancelled, or no longer true.

## Budget

Monthly income after tax, fixed costs, and the targets the person set for each category.

## Goals

Each goal with its amount, target date, and progress.

## Debts

Each debt with its balance, interest rate, minimum payment, and the date of those figures.

## Bills

Each recurring bill with its amount, due day, and whether a reminder is set.

## Assumptions

Rates used in plans, such as inflation or a savings rate, each with its source and date.

## Notes

The `memory` tool appends here.
